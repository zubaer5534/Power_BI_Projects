## Project Overview
Analyzed 6 months of Kent Natural Store data to find insights and the make a dashboard for the stakeholders.

​

● Built a multi-page report using Power BI to help in taking business decisions by focusing on products and inventory.

● Forecasted the effect of price change from 0.25 cents to 1.5 dollars for profit margins.

● Identified longest perishable items, which were 3.15% for better efficiency in inventory and preventing wastage

● Analyzed buyer-level performance to improve inventory allocation and sales strategy.
